Celebrity Heads

How to play

  1. Every player gets a name that everyone else can see, except them.
  2. Pass the phone around. Show each screen to the whole group, but keep it hidden from the one player it belongs to.
  3. Once everyone's seen every name but their own, take turns asking yes/no questions about your own name.
  4. Keep asking until you guess who you are, then you're out.
  5. No objects needed, just the phone going around once.
