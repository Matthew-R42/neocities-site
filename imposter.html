Imposter

How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same secret word, except one player.
  2. That player sees only the word IMPOSTER and the category.
  3. Go round the circle. Each player says one clue about the word.
  4. The imposter has to bluff a clue that fits the category.
  5. Vote on who the imposter is. Civilians win if they get it right.
  6. The imposter wins by surviving, or by guessing the secret word.
