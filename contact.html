How to play

  1. One player is picked as the Thinker and gets a secret word.
  2. The Thinker says the word's first letter out loud.
  3. Guessers call out a word starting with those letters and give a clue for it.
  4. If the Thinker knows what the guesser is thinking of, they say "no contact" and play continues. If another guesser figures out the same word first, they shout "contact" before the Thinker can respond, and that letter is confirmed.
  5. Once the whole word is spelled out this way, guessers win. The Thinker wins by never getting caught out.
