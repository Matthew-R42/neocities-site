Game Ideas

No app needed for these, just people and sometimes paper. Kept here so the list survives past whichever night I thought of it.

Celebrity Heads
Everyone writes a well-known name on a slip of paper. Slips get shuffled and stuck to each player's forehead, unseen by them, and each person asks yes/no questions to work out who they are. Played this one recently, still holds up.
Mafia / Werewolf
A moderator secretly assigns a few players as the Mafia, everyone else as townsfolk. The group debates by day to vote someone out; the Mafia quietly picks off a townsfolk each night. Needs six or more people and a moderator willing to sit out.
Codenames
Two teams, one-word clues. Each team's spymaster gives a single word and a number, trying to get their team to point at their agents on a shared grid of words without hitting the other team's or the assassin. Rewards precise, lateral thinking.
Wavelength
A hidden dial sits somewhere between two opposing concepts, like "overrated / underrated." One player gives a clue aimed at the hidden point, and the rest of the group guesses where the needle sits. Good for a mixed group since there's no wrong answer, only close ones.
Charades
Act out a word or phrase with no talking and no props while your team guesses against the clock. Splits into teams, works from a phone timer and any category list.
Two Truths and a Lie
Each player states three things about themselves, two true and one invented, and the group votes on which is the lie. Zero setup, good icebreaker for a group that doesn't know each other well.
Never Have I Ever
Go around saying something you've never done; anyone who has done it puts a finger down (or takes a drink, if that's the version being played). Escalates fast once people start fishing for specific answers.
Fishbowl
Everyone writes a few names, places, or things on slips and drops them in a bowl. Play three rounds with the same slips: describe, then act out, then say a single word, so later rounds get harder as the words get familiar.
Contact
One player thinks of a secret word and gives its first letter. Others try to guess the word by giving a clue for a word that starts with the same letters revealed so far; if the thinker figures out the guesser's word before someone else shouts "contact," the guess is blocked. Fast, verbal, no materials.
Taboo
Get your team to say a target word without using it or five related words printed on the card. Needs a deck (or a phone list of word/taboo-word sets) and a timer.
Wits & Wagers style trivia
Ask numeric trivia questions nobody actually knows the answer to, everyone writes a guess, then bets on whose guess is closest. Turns "nobody knows this" into the fun part instead of a dead end.
Scattergories
A letter and a list of categories, everyone has a set time to write one answer per category starting with that letter. Score a point for any answer nobody else wrote, so overlap kills points.
