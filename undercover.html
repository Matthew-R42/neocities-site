Undercover

How to Play

  1. Everyone except one person gets the same word
  2. The Undercover gets a different (but related) word
  3. Go around the circle - each player gives ONE clue about their word
  4. After each round, vote on who you think is the Undercover
  5. Undercover wins if they survive until 3 or fewer players remain
  6. Civilians win if they eliminate the Undercover
Player 1

Pass the device around. Each player taps "Show Word" to see their word privately, then "Hide Word" before passing to the next player.
